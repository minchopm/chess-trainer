How this is made

Facts, an engine, and nothing else.

The moves and results come from the events’ official live broadcasts, as relayed by Lichess. A record of which moves were played is a fact. Every assessment of a position is Stockfish’s, and each story says so.

The words are written from those two things only: no quotes, no guesses about what anybody was thinking, and no photographs — the boards are drawn by Brass Pawn itself. Brass Pawn is not affiliated with the events, the federations or the players.
